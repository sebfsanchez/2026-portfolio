Future Frequencies

Future Frequencies: Explorations in Generative Art and Fashion was a Christie's x Gucci auction on Christie's 3.0, Christie's on-chain platform, from 18 to 25 July 2023. Its 21 works by artists including Claire Silver, Tyler Hobbs, Emily Xie, Botto, William Mapan and Zach Lieberman drew on Gucci's archive, textiles and the Bamboo 1947 collection, alongside Dani Loftus's DRAUP digital gown Antheia, billed as the first digital fashion piece sold at a major auction house. A second chapter, Parallel Universes: From Future Frequencies to Gucci Cosmos, followed in November 2023.

The auction features a select group of artists heavily inspired by the design and production of garments, textiles and the fashion industry at large. The outputs they've created range from abstract to literal and are completely fascinating, changing the way we can think about using algorithms and data to advance human creativity across fields. Sebastian Sanchez, Manager of Digital Art Sales, Christie's
