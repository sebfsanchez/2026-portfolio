On NFTs

On NFTs was a collaborative exhibition and publication with TASCHEN exploring the history and cultural significance of NFTs and digital art. The project brought together leading voices in the space to examine the movement's impact on art and collecting. The US launch of the book was held at Christie's, coinciding with a generative drop by Robert Alice through Christie's 3.0 — marking the first time in the auction house's history that it conducted a Dutch auction with generative minting on-chain.
